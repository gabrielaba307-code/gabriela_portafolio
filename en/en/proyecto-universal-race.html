Horse Racing Platform · Web + Mobile · UX/UI

Understand the race before placing a bet.

Universal Race brings together races, participants, and markets that need to be interpreted quickly. The UX/UI work focused on making alternatives easier to read and compare across desktop and mobile.

Role UX/UI Designer
Product Horse Racing Platform
Versions Web · Mobile
Focus Responsive UX/UI
01 · The Race

Every event brings several variables together.

Before making a selection, users need to identify the race, review participants, and find the betting options they are interested in.

The design needed to support a transition from an overall view to a specific decision.
