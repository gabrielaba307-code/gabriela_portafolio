My Process

From a problem to a functional experience.

Each project starts by understanding users and business needs and turning them into clear and intuitive solutions.

1

Discover

I analyze the problem, business goals, user needs, and product requirements.

2

Define

I organize findings, prioritize needs, and define architecture, flows, and functionality.

3

Design

I create wireframes, interfaces, and interactive prototypes with a focus on usability and visual consistency.

4

Validate & Iterate

I review the solution, identify opportunities for improvement, and refine the product before handoff.
