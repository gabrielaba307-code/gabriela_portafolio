Sportsbook · Web Platform · UX/UI

Explore, select, and build your bet slip.

Aciértala brings together sports, leagues, events, markets, and odds within a sportsbook with an extensive offering. The design connects match exploration, market selection, and bet slip building without losing the selections made during the session.

Role UX/UI Designer
Product Sportsbook
Experience Sports Betting
Platform Web · Desktop
01 · Betting Session

Many options before a single bet.

A user may start by browsing football, enter a league, compare several matches, open additional markets, and combine selections from different events.

The challenge appears when exploration starts competing with the bet the user is already building.

The interface needed to let users investigate new alternatives without losing sight of which selections were already part of the bet slip.

02 · The Challenge

Explore without losing what you selected.

The sportsbook needed to manage an extensive offering without forcing users to start over every time they moved between sports, leagues, or matches.

01

Find events

Organize sports, leagues, and matches to reduce the effort required to search.

02

Discover markets

Move from common odds to more specific betting alternatives within an event.

03

Preserve selections

Keep chosen options available while users review other matches and markets.

04 · Selection Flow

A selection should not disappear.

Once users enter a match, the interface moves away from broad discovery and focuses on the event and its markets.

Teams, score, and match status help users understand the event before reviewing more specific categories.

When an odd is selected, it is immediately added to the bet slip. Users can continue exploring while keeping a permanent reference to what they have already chosen.

As selections are added, the bet slip brings together the market, odds, stake, and potential return before confirmation.

05 · UX/UI Decisions

Design for an entire betting session.

The decisions aim to ensure that exploring new events does not mean forgetting previous selections or losing clarity about what will eventually be confirmed.

01

Persistent bet slip

Selections remain available while users explore other events.

02

Actionable odds

Available options and selected states are visually differentiated.

03

Market depth

The event list surfaces common betting options while event detail expands the available alternatives.

04

Event status

Score and match time receive stronger visual priority when the event is live.

05

Immediate feedback

Selected odds change state and appear immediately inside the bet slip.

06

Review before betting

Selections, total odds, stake, and potential return can be checked before confirmation.

06 · Outcome

A session that preserves every decision.

The solution connects exploration, event detail, and the bet slip instead of treating them as isolated workflows.

Users can review multiple alternatives while the platform preserves previous selections and clearly shows what will eventually be confirmed as a bet.
